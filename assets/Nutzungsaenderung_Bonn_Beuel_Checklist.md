## 1. Executive Summary

We are preparing a formal **Antrag auf Baugenehmigung für eine Nutzungsänderung** (simplified procedure under **§ 64 BauO NRW**) with the **Bauordnungsamt der Bundesstadt Bonn** for the property in **53229 Bonn-Beuel**.

* **Current Approved Use:** Pure residential building (*Zweifamilienhaus* with existing approved residential units from the 1961 building permit).
* **Proposed Use:** Mixed residential and short-term holiday accommodation (*Ferienwohnung* / small *Beherbergungseinheit*) on the 1st floor (approx. 70 m², 3 rooms, max. 4–6 guests), with the Ground Floor and converted Dachgeschoss remaining owner-occupied residential space.
* **Key Approval Issues & High Risks:**
  1. **Unapproved Structural Wall Removal:** A load-bearing wall was previously removed to create an open kitchen. Because this structural change is not shown in the historic 1961 *Baugenehmigung*, it must be legally regularized in this application with structural verification (*Statische Unbedenklichkeitsbescheinigung / Tragwerksnachweis*).
  2. **Stellplatznachweis (Parking Space Legalization):** The 1961 permit legally shows only 1 parking space. The second space built in 2024 is physically present but not formally approved. The submission must legally document and certify **both** parking spaces under the current Bonn Stellplatz rules.
  3. **Active Neighbor Complaints:** Existing disputes with neighbors mean the Bauamt will inspect distance areas (*Abstandsflächen*), noise immissions, and exact zoning compliance under **§ 34 BauGB** with heightened scrutiny.
  4. **Zoning Classification:** Under § 34 BauGB (likely *Reines Wohngebiet* [WR] or *Allgemeines Wohngebiet* [WA]), small Ferienwohnungen run by resident owner-hosts are permissible (in WA by right under § 4 BauNVO; in WR as an exception under § 31 BauGB / § 3 BauNVO).

All technical drawings must be prepared by a qualified architect (*Bauvorlageberechtigter* per § 67 BauO NRW) in compliance with the **BauPrüfVO NRW**:

* **Grundriss 1. Obergeschoss (1:100):**

  * **Color coding for past structural modification:** Yellow = removed load-bearing wall; Red = installed structural support (e.g., HEA/HEB steel beam or concrete lintel).
  * Position of optical smoke alarms (*Rauchwarnmelder nach DIN 14676*).
  * Clear marking of the primary escape route door and the designated 2nd escape route window (*Notausstiegsfenster: lichte Breite ≥ 0,90 m, lichte Höhe ≥ 1,20 m, Brüstungshöhe ≤ 1,20 m*).

  * Shows the common internal concrete staircase leading unobstructed directly to the ground-floor exit door to the outside.
* **Schnittzeichnung / Building Section (1:100):**
  * Shows clear ceiling heights (≥ 2.40 m per § 46 BauO NRW), floor construction, and height of the top floor sill above ground level to confirm **Gebäudeklasse 2** (< 7.00 m).
* **Lageplan & Stellplatzplan (1:500 / 1:200):**

## 5. Stellplatz Analysis (Bonn Regulations)

* **Legal Baseline:** The 1961 building permit required and legally secured **1 parking space**. In 2024, a second physical space was added without formal building approval.
* **Required Key Figures (Stadt Bonn Stellplatzsatzung / Verwaltungspraxis):**
  * *Ground Floor Residential Unit:* Requires **1.0 Stellplatz**.
  * *Ferienwohnung (approx. 70 m² / up to 4–6 beds):* Calculated either as residential equivalent (1.0 space) or as small accommodation (1 space per unit/every 4 beds). Total requirement: **1.0 Stellplatz**.
  * *Total Property Obligation:* **2.0 Stellplätze**.
* **Status of Compliance:**
  * The property currently has 2 physical spaces in the front setback, both independently accessible from the street.
  * **Action for Submission:** The architect must formally include the 2024 second space in the new *Lageplan* and *Stellplatznachweis*. Dimension lines (minimum 2.50 m width × 5.00 m length, unhindered access without blocking the sidewalk) must be explicitly shown.
  * *Verdict:* **The 2 parking spaces are fully sufficient**, provided the structural layout complies with § 48 BauO NRW and municipal curb-drop (*Bürgersteigabsenkung*) regulations. A *Stellplatzablösung* (cash buy-out) will **not** be required.

## 6. Fire Safety Analysis (Brandschutz & Rettungswege)

* **Building Classification:** **Gebäudeklasse 2** (§ 2 Abs. 3 BauO NRW: building up to 7 m height, not more than 2 units, ≤ 400 m² total floor area).
* **Formal Requirement:** A complex *Brandschutzkonzept* by a certified fire safety expert is **NOT legally required** for Gebäudeklasse 2. Standard fire safety measures within the architectural drawings (*Bautechnischer Nachweis / Brandschutz im Bauantrag*) are sufficient.
* **1st Escape Route (*1. Rettungsweg*):**
  * From 1. OG apartment door through the existing massive concrete internal staircase down to the ground floor entrance directly into the open air.
* **2nd Escape Route (*2. Rettungsweg*):**
  * Designated rescue window on the 1st floor facing the front street/parking area.
  * *Requirements:* Clear opening dimensions of at least **0.90 m width × 1.20 m height**, maximum sill height (*Brüstungshöhe*) of **1.20 m** above finished floor level. The front parking/driveway area must remain unobstructed to allow ladder access (*Anleiterung*) by the Bonn Fire Department.
* **Smoke Alarms:** Mandatory installation according to **§ 49 BauO NRW** in all bedrooms and the central apartment hallway, indicated clearly on the architect's floor plan.

## 7. Planning-Law Analysis (§ 34 BauGB / Bebauungsplan)

* **Written Proof of Planning Status:** If no B-Plan exists, you do not receive a negative certificate. Instead, your architect states in the application that the property is situated within an **unbeplanter Innenbereich gemäß § 34 BauGB**. (To verify the official boundary, check the *Bonn Geoportal / Masterportal* online or request a *Flurkartenauszug* from the Katasteramt).
* **Zoning Compatibility (§ 34 BauGB in conjunction with BauNVO):**
  * *Allgemeines Wohngebiet (WA - § 4 BauNVO):* Ferienwohnungen are permitted by right (*allgemein zulässig*) as non-disruptive commercial lodging units (*kleine Beherbergungsbetriebe*) or residential equivalents.
  * *Reines Wohngebiet (WR - § 3 BauNVO):* In pure residential zones, commercial short-term accommodation can be permitted exceptionally (*ausnahmsweise zulässig nach § 31 Abs. 1 BauGB*) if operated quietly by the resident owner without commercial catering or exterior advertising.
* **Neighbor Protection (*Gebot der Rücksichtnahme*):** Because active complaints exist, the application explicitly mitigates noise risks by formally documenting in the *Betriebsbeschreibung* that parties/events are banned, max capacity is capped, check-ins are monitored by on-site resident hosts, and 1 parking spot is reserved strictly off-street.

* **Query 4:** *"Please demonstrate how the second escape route for guests on the 1st floor is secured."*
  * *Preventive Solution:* Clearly label the designated rescue window on the 1. OG floor plan with width, height, and sill measurements, showing ladder clearance down to the driveway.
